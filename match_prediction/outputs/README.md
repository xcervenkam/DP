# Outputs

This folder contains the final tables and figures prepared for the thesis. They are generated by notebook `08_thesis_outputs_cs.ipynb`.

## Tables

- `tab_data_a_casove_rozdeleni.csv` describes the development, validation, and final test periods.
- `tab_vybrane_modely_1x2.csv` lists the selected structural and market 1X2 models.
- `tab_predikce_zapasu_1x2.csv` compares the final match-prediction results across the five leagues.
- `tab_predikce_sezon.csv` summarises the quality of the season predictions before the season and at the final in-season simulation point.

## Figures

- `obr_vyvoj_kumulativni_presnosti_1x2.pdf` shows how cumulative 1X2 prediction accuracy developed during the 2024/25 season.
- `obr_ligue1_matice_zamen_kalibrace.pdf` shows the confusion matrix and probability calibration for the Ligue 1 model.
- `obr_vyvoj_sezonnich_metrik.pdf` compares the season-prediction measures at the four simulation points.
- `obr_ligue1_intervaly_poradi.pdf` shows the predicted position intervals for Ligue 1 during the season.

The Czech file names and labels correspond to the terminology used in the thesis.
