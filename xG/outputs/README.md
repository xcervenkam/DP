# Outputs

This folder contains the final figures and tables selected for the thesis. The files are created in `xG_thesis_outputs_cs.ipynb` and use Czech labels.

Only outputs used in the final text are included. Temporary figures, preview versions, PNG files and LaTeX table files are not stored here.

## Figures

The figures are saved in PDF format:

- `casovy_vyvoj_top5_ma490_zapasy.pdf` – development of expected goals and actual goals across the five analysed leagues.

- `casovy_vyvoj_la_liga_ma100_zapasy.pdf` – development of expected goals and actual goals in La Liga.

- `leicester_body_xpts.pdf` – comparison of actual and expected points in the 2015/16 Premier League season.

- `leicester_goly_xg.pdf` – comparison of actual and expected goals in the 2015/16 Premier League season.

- `leicester_obdrzene_goly_xga.pdf` – comparison of actual and expected goals conceded in the 2015/16 Premier League season.

- `leicester_dbscan_pressing.pdf` – DBSCAN clustering of Premier League teams based on their pressing profiles.

- `hierarchicke_shlukovani_epl_2015.pdf` – hierarchical clustering of Premier League teams in the 2015/16 season.

## Tables

The numerical results are saved in CSV format:

- `tabulka_ligy_na_zapas.csv` – comparison of expected and actual goals by league;
- `tabulka_neuplna_sezona_2019_20.csv` – incomplete league-seasons in 2019/20;
- `tabulka_utocne_nad_ocekavani.csv` – teams with the highest attacking ratio;
- `tabulka_obranne_nad_ocekavani.csv` – teams with the lowest defensive ratio;
- `tabulka_epl_2015_16.csv` – Premier League table with expected metrics;
- `tabulka_dbscan_pressing.csv` – DBSCAN cluster membership;
- `tabulka_hierarchicke_shluky.csv` – hierarchical cluster membership;
- `tabulka_hierarchicke_profily.csv` – average values of variables in the hierarchical clusters.
